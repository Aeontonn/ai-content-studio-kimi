# Brand profile: Calm Habits (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)

> Example of a finished onboarding. The real profile lives in
> content-studio/brand/brand-profile.md and starts as a blank template.

## 1. Basics
- **Brand / creator name:** Calm Habits
- **Instagram handle:** @calmhabits.sample
- **Other channels:** TikTok @calmhabits.sample · YouTube Shorts · weekly newsletter (Thursdays)
- **Content language(s):** English captions and on-screen text
- **Audience timezone:** Europe/Stockholm
- **Location / market:** Nordics and UK, English-speaking

## 2. Niche and positioning
- **Niche in one sentence:** I help busy people build calm, realistic morning and evening habits without 5 a.m. alarms or 90-minute routines.
- **What makes this account different:** anti-hustle; small habits that survive bad days; honest about failing.
- **Credibility:** certified health coach; rebuilt their own routine after burnout; 3 years of coaching clients.

## 3. Target audience
- **Primary persona:** 25-40, office or hybrid job, often parents of small children, tired of productivity content that makes them feel behind.
- **Main problems:** routines collapse within a week; mornings feel rushed; phone first thing; dark winters.
- **What they want:** calmer mornings, feeling in control, small wins, less guilt.
- **Objections:** "I'm not a morning person", "I don't have time", "I've tried everything".
- **Words they use:** "chaos", "I always snooze", "my mornings are a mess", "I just want 10 minutes to myself".
- **Awareness:** problem-aware; skeptical of big promises.

## 4. Goals and offers
- **Main goal (next 3 months):** grow the newsletter to 2,000 subscribers; secondary: saves and shares.
- **Offers:** free "10-minute morning" guide (newsletter sign-up); 4-week habit course in February.
- **Primary CTA:** save / share · **Secondary CTA:** free guide (link in bio)

## 5. Content pillars
| Pillar | What it covers | Share | Example topics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Habits | practical routines, tiny habits, systems | 40% | 10-minute routines, habit stacking, phone-down time |
| Slow living | calm, seasonal, cozy, subtraction | 25% | dark mornings, Sunday reset, stop lists |
| Mindset | self-talk, guilt, consistency over perfection | 20% | "miss once, never twice", rest is productive |
| Behind the scenes | real mornings, filming, failures | 15% | real-time routines, Q&As |

## 6. Voice and tone
- **Three words:** warm, honest, calm
- **We sound like:** a friend who has been there and figured out the realistic version
- **We never sound like:** a drill sergeant, a guru, an ad
- **Do:** short sentences; "you"; gentle humor; admit failures; specific numbers (10 minutes, one song)
- **Don't:** hype words ("crush", "hustle", "game-changer"), guilt, all caps, more than 1 emoji per caption
- **Signature phrases:** "the version that survives a bad day", "miss once, never twice"
- **Words to avoid:** grind, hack, optimize, 5 a.m. club (unless critiquing it)

## 7. Visual identity
- **Aesthetic:** calm, warm, natural light, deep green + cream + terracotta
- **Photo / video style:** kitchen counter and window, mugs, notebooks, real mess allowed
- **On-screen text:** lowercase, one key word highlighted in terracotta
- **Reference accounts:** (style only) soft, natural-light lifestyle creators

## 8. Formats and series
- **Recurring series:** Monday check-in poll (Stories) · Friday Q&A (Stories)
- **Natural formats:** talking head, voice-over B-roll, real-time routines, list carousels
- **Avoid:** dancing trends, pointing-at-text trends
- **Filming constraints:** batch-films Tuesday mornings; about 2 hours per week

## 9. Boundaries
- **Off-limits:** children's faces, home address, diet talk, weight loss
- **Claims to avoid:** medical claims about sleep or hormones

## 10. Hashtags and keywords
- **Core:** #morningroutine #slowliving #habits
- **Keywords:** morning routine, slow morning, habits for busy people, 10-minute routine

## 11. Performance learnings
- 2026-11-10: list carousels with a recap slide get about 2x the saves of single-idea carousels.
- 2026-11-14: Reels whose hook names a feeling ("chaos") beat Reels whose hook names a tip.
